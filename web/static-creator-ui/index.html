    Quality Assurance

    How output is controlled against the three objectives

    Engagement assurance

    1. Platform and audience are captured before generation.
    2. Hook, title options, retention beats, pacing, and storyboard are produced as separate reviewable artifacts.
    3. Creative QA checks specificity, clarity, first-seconds strength, and production feasibility.
    4. Human feedback triggers revision and before/after comparison.
    Evidence: P42, P50, P51, P52, P53

    Policy and rights assurance

    1. Brief captures avoid rules and source notes.
    2. Rights engine generates copyright, likeness, logo, music, asset, and claims cautions.
    3. QA confirms owned or licensed production inputs.
    4. No final approval or publishing occurs automatically.
    Evidence: P41, P50, P56, human approval gate

    Monetization assurance

    1. Monetization objective is captured at brief stage.
    2. CTA, format, duration, platform notes, and conversion path are aligned.
    3. Producer exports make execution consistent and measurable.
    4. No revenue, views, savings, or conversion outcomes are guaranteed.
    Evidence: P44, P46, P48, P49

    Current Product Position

    • Deployed Vercel interface is static and browser-only.
    • Full P40–P61 processing is implemented as local Python modules and runners.
    • No database, automatic publishing, final approval, or external AI call is active in this static deployment.
    • Browser output is a first-pass planning pack, not a final video and not a monetization guarantee.
    • Human review remains required before production.
