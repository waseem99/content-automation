# P42 Engagement and Retention Optimization Engine

Part of #507. Closes #508–#513 after merge.

## What this builds

P42 evaluates a generated video package for engagement quality before production.

## Output

- hook diagnostics
- first-three-second strength score
- retention curve estimate
- pacing analysis
- emotional, novelty, share, save, CTA, and rewatch scores
- improvement plan
- alternate hooks
- CTA variants
- final engagement scorecard

## Safety boundary

No live analytics ingestion, scraping, publishing, upload, or guaranteed performance claims.
