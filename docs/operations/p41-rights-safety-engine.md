# P41 Rights and Monetization Safety Intelligence Engine

Part of #499. Closes #500–#505 after merge.

## What this builds

P41 analyzes a P40 video package before production and tells the operator whether it is safe to proceed, needs revision, or should be blocked.

## Output

- rights risk inventory
- safer rewrite suggestions
- asset/source manifest
- originality and transformation notes
- platform monetization safety flags
- safety scores
- final publish gate

## Safety boundary

P41 is not legal advice. It does not claim legal clearance, scrape rights databases, call external APIs, download assets, upload, render, or publish.
