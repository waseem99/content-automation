# Evidence contract

Label every claim as `measured`, `extracted`, `model_observation`, or `fallback`. Preserve
provenance and confidence. For video, report hook, setup, development, payoff, and CTA only from
sequence-level evidence. For images and carousels, report visual hierarchy and slide progression.
Never infer a complete story from a thumbnail or single frame, and never call a still/slideshow an
animated video.

Persist the normalized manifest, temporal/carousel map, creative fingerprint, originality-safe
brief, limitations, and next action. Exclude source bytes, source excerpts, credentials, absolute
paths, identities, logos, watermarks, voices, music, and protected expression. Require human rights
and originality review before production.
