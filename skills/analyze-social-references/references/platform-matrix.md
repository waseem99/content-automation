|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube / Shorts | Supported through extractor | Conditional | Authorized local file |

Public availability is not a permanent guarantee. Do not bypass private-account controls, DRM,
paywalls, CAPTCHAs, rate limits, or platform safeguards. Discover candidates before acquiring
profile/page inputs and report partial pagination or authentication constraints honestly.
