# animal-prairie-dog-alarm — Original Narration

One prairie dog's call can change how an entire colony reacts to danger. The call is not simply noise. Its pattern can vary with the kind of threat detected. Changes in pitch and timing carry information through the colony. Nearby animals become vigilant and choose cover according to what they detect. Because many animals can hear and answer, warning spreads beyond the first lookout. The result is a warning system built from attention, sound, and coordinated behavior.
