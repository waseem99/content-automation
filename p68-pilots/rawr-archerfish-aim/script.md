# rawr-archerfish-aim — Original Narration

This fish hits a target above water even though the surface makes it appear displaced. Light changes direction when it crosses from air into water, shifting the insect's apparent position. The archerfish learns the relationship between what it sees and where the target really is. Then its mouth shapes a fast water jet aimed through the surface. The jet strengthens toward the front, concentrating the impact near the target. It is not a lucky shot. The fish combines optics, learned calibration, and precise fluid control.
