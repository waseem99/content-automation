# animal-elephant-signals — Original Narration

This elephant may be listening to a message you cannot hear, through the ground. A deep rumble does not travel only through air. Some energy enters the soil as vibration. The signal can move across the landscape while the receiving elephant stands completely still. Sensitive feet and body pathways can help turn that vibration into useful information. Studies suggest elephants can distinguish information in these signals, including who produced a call. When a herd suddenly pauses, the conversation may already be beneath them.
