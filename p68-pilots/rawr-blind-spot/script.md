# rawr-blind-spot — Original Narration

There is a hole in your vision right now, and you almost never notice it. Inside each eye, light lands on a retina packed with cells that detect it. But one small spot has no visual cells at all. That is where the optic nerve leaves the eye, carrying signals toward the brain. Close one eye during a dot test, and the missing mark can disappear completely. Your brain fills the gap from the surrounding pattern. Seeing is partly reconstruction.
